What is OpenCupid?

The connections your community needs, waiting to be found

Within every circle — whether that's a collective, association, ecovillage, or shared-interest group — there are countless valuable connections waiting to happen. Yet many remain hidden because existing platforms don't facilitate discovery and members are often scattered.

OpenCupid helps surface these connections in a safe and intentional way, facilitating their evolution into meaningful bonds — whether that's friendship, collaboration, or romantic partnership — within the circles you already belong to.

Features

Everything a community needs to connect

A complete matchmaking toolkit — no subscriptions, no upsells, no hidden costs.

Browse & Match

Members create profiles and discover others based on shared interests, values, and mutual preferences. Connections happen on both sides' terms.

Messaging & Voice

Real-time chat and voice calls built in. Once you've matched, communicate naturally without switching to another app.

Bulletin Board

A community bulletin board where members can post updates, announcements, and requests — keeping everyone in the loop.

Map View

See where community members are located on an interactive map. Great for finding nearby people or planning meetups.

Push Notifications

Keep members engaged with timely notifications for new matches, messages, and community activity.

Multilingual

Built with internationalization from day one. Currently available in English and Hungarian, with more languages coming from the community.

Privacy & Trust

Built on respect, not exploitation

Unlike commercial matchmaking apps, OpenCupid is free from dark patterns and manipulative design. Just a simple, transparent tool that puts your community first.

Reciprocal Visibility

You only see details others choose to share if you've shared the same information yourself, and only when your preferences match theirs.

Explicit Consent

Nobody can approach you unless you've explicitly given permission. Connections grow on mutual terms.

No Dark Patterns

No hidden algorithms trying to keep you hooked, no upsells, and no data exploitation. Transparent and honest by design.

Community-Owned Data

Self-host on your own infrastructure. Your community's data stays with your community — never sold, never shared.
